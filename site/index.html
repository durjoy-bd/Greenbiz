How it works

From the shelf to a loan offer.

Every record the shop already makes builds a clear, honest picture of the business — one step feeding the next.

Stock

Add products by hand or from a photo, down to 0.001 kg.

Sale

Tap, type or say it. Stock drops in the same atomic step.

Payment SMS

bKash, Nagad, Rocket and Upay messages read on the phone.

Linked receipt

The right message is attached to the sale and counted once.

Credit profile

A 0–100 score from four factors anyone can understand.

Loan plans

39 real products sized so the instalment stays affordable.

Selling

Ring up a sale in seconds — by touch, text or voice.

Say “2 kg rice and 3 soap” in English or Bangla and GreenBiz builds the cart. Nothing is guessed: unclear items are left for you to pick.

  • Swipe to confirm: right for cash, left for mobile money.
  • Exact to the poisha — loose goods sold by weight with three decimals.
  • Never double-counted: a retried sale can't take stock twice.
Mobile money

The right bKash message, matched to the right sale.

Payment confirmations land a minute after the customer pays — so GreenBiz shows the last ten minutes first, closest amount first.

  • Understands cash-out charges: a receipt within 2% of the bill (at least ৳1) is the same payment.
  • Live alerts: “bKash payment received · Tap to create a sale”, with the amount hidden on the lock screen.
  • Business or personal with one swipe; personal money stays out of reports.
Trusted senders only — exact short codes like 16247
Reports

Know exactly how the business is doing.

Net cash flow, money in and out, average sale, best day, top payer and payment-method mix — compared with the previous period.

  • Calendar periods in Dhaka time. Weeks start on Saturday.
  • Linked receipts count once, so totals never inflate.
  • CSV export with payer and reference for the accountant.
Credit & loans

A credit profile the owner can actually read.

Four factors, each explained in one sentence — and a plan to improve the weakest one. Then 39 published SME loans, sized to what the shop can repay.

  • Consistency 30% · Volume 25% · Stability 25% · Activity 20% over 90 days.
  • Affordable by design: the instalment stays under half of monthly revenue.
  • High priority, Good fit or Stretch — with requirements and warnings for every plan.
AI assistant

AI help with nothing to set up.

Snap a shelf to draft stock entries, describe a sale, or ask for advice. GreenBiz's managed service handles it — no key, account or server for the owner.

  • Always a draft: every suggestion is reviewed before it's saved.
  • Totals only for advice — never SMS, names or phone numbers.
  • Safe to run: per-device and daily limits, and nothing is stored.

Privacy & security

Honest by design.

GreenBiz handles people's money and messages, so it is built to keep them on the phone and to never claim more than it knows.

SMS never leaves the phone

Messages are parsed on the device. Only amount, method and reference are kept; phone numbers are masked to the last four digits.

No fake “verified”

A TrxID is a customer's claim, not proof. Nothing in the app can mark a payment verified without a real provider integration.

Keystore-encrypted secrets

Session tokens and AI credentials are encrypted with an Android Keystore key. Servers store only SHA-256 hashes.

Consent before banks

Lenders see a shop only after the owner turns sharing on — and only totals and scores, never customers or stock.

Hardened server

bcrypt passwords, email confirmation, sign-in lockouts, HttpOnly SameSite cookies, origin checks, strict schemas and two-step sign-in for administrators.

Exactly-once writes

Every sale and offline change carries a request ID, so a lost connection or a retry can never duplicate money or stock.

Technology

One domain engine, everywhere.

The phone, the browser and the server all run the same TypeScript code to change records — so they can never disagree about stock or totals.
